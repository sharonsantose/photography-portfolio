🔬 Research-Backed Productivity Principles

1. Zeigarnik Effect & Masicampo-Baumeister (2011)

Uncompleted tasks clutter working memory. Making a concrete plan (when, where, how) eliminates intrusive thoughts.

2. Hick's Law & Rule of 3 (Hick 1952)

Decision time increases logarithmically with options. Daily focus is capped at 3 items to avoid paralysis.

3. Planning Fallacy & 1.5x Buffer (Kahneman 1979)

Humans systematically underestimate completion time. CogniDo applies an empirical 1.5x buffer.

4. Implementation Intentions (Gollwitzer 1999)

Formatting tasks as If [Trigger], Then [Action] increases completion rates by 2–3x.

5. Goal-Gradient Effect (Hull 1932)

Motivation accelerates near task completion. Visual progress bars trigger dopamine momentum.
