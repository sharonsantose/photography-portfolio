📋 Weekly Reasoning & Focus Breakdown

Week 1 (Jul 27 – Aug 2): Foundational Aerobic Ramp & 4-Swim Habituation

Reasoning: Establish the every-other-day running cadence, initial 5.0 mi Long Run (Sun Jul 26/Aug 2), 3x Full Body lifts, and 4 Swims (Mon/Wed/Fri/Sat). Low-stress Zone 2 adaptation prevents early shin splints and keeps cortisol low.

Week 2 (Aug 3 – Aug 9): Speed Introduction & Muscle Glucose Sink Build

Reasoning: Step Long Run to 6.5 miles. Introduce Broken Miles tempo intervals (4x1km @ 9:45/mi pace) to elevate VO2 max without glycogen depletion. 45m Smith Machine lifts maximize GLUT4 muscle glucose storage.

Week 3 (Aug 10 – Aug 16): Mid-Block Consolidation & Swim Pace Intervals

Reasoning: Consolidate mileage with an 8.0 mi Long Run. Focus on 6x100yd swim pace intervals @ 1:50/100yd across 4 swim sessions. Allows connective tissue to absorb early mileage gains while maintaining 4x weekly swim conditioning.

Week 4 (Aug 17 – Aug 23): Double-Digit Stamina & Arm Toning Surge

Reasoning: First double-digit Long Run (10.0 miles). Pyramid speed intervals @ 9:30/mi pace + intensive cable bicep/tricep supersets. Reaching 10 miles builds mental toughness for half marathon distance.

Week 5 (Aug 24 – Aug 30): Peak Aerobic Endurance & Fueling Protocol Test

Reasoning: 11.5 mi Practice Long Run + Peak 25-mile Zone 2 Bike Ride. Practice taking Maurten Gels at Min 45 & 90 to train gut absorption under fatigue and prevent late-race bonking.

Week 6 (Aug 31 – Sep 6): 13.1 Mile Milestone Peak & Mental Mastery

Reasoning: EXECUTE THE 13.1 MILE FULL HALF MARATHON PRACTICE RUN (Sat Sep 5) at slow party pace (11:30–12:00/mi). Running the full 13.1 miles 2 weeks prior provides absolute mental confidence and proves complete race readiness.

Week 7 (Sep 7 – Sep 13): Sharpening & Taper Phase 1

Reasoning: Reduce Long Run to 6.0 miles. Maintain sharp 3.1 mi easy runs and 4 light swim technique sessions. Tapering reduces systemic fatigue while short sessions keep neuromuscular sharp.

Week 8 (Sep 14 – Sep 20): Race Week Peak, Carb Load & Orca Half Marathon

Reasoning: Easy 3.1 mi shakeout runs, 4 light swim floats, carbohydrate loading (pasta/rice dinner), 3L hydration, and RACE DAY! Target pace: 10:18/mi for a strong finish!

🐋 Orca Half Marathon Training Plan

8-Week Master Calendar • Mon Jul 27 to Sun Sep 20, 2026

⏱ Gym/Swim Days (Mon/Wed/Fri): 6:00 AM – 7:30 AM  |  🏃 Run Days (Tue/Thu): 6:45 AM – 7:30 AM  |  🚿 Shower & Prep: 7:30 AM – 8:15 AM  |  🚗 Leave Home: 8:15 AM

🧠 Master Training Philosophy

  • Every-Other-Day Running Cadence: Spacing runs with non-impact swim/spin days protects joints and shins from continuous impact loading.
  • 4 Swims per Week (32 Sessions Total): Mon (Glide), Wed (Pace Intervals), Fri (600yd Continuous), Sat/Sun (Recovery Float).
  • PCOS Cortisol & Fat Loss Engine: 80% Zone 2 running (11:45–12:15/mi) burns body fat as primary fuel without spiking stress hormones.
  • Muscle Glucose Sink (3x/wk Full Body Lifts): 45m Smith Machine & Cable lifts (Mon/Wed/Fri) with dedicated Bicep & Tricep Arm Toning supersets.
  • 13.1 Mile Milestone Peak: Full 13.1 Mile Half Marathon Practice Run executed on Saturday, Sept 5 for complete race readiness.
