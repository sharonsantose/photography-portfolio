📋 Weekly Reasoning & Focus Breakdown

Week 0 (Aug 6 – Aug 9): Partial Ramp-In & Session Habituation

Reasoning: Introductory 1-2 set lifts, 3 mi easy run with strides, 6.0 mi long run, and long outdoor ride to establish morning/evening session rhythms.

Week 1 (Aug 10 – Aug 16): Initial Build & Full Gym Sessions

Reasoning: Establish 4 mi easy run + 4 mi tempo run structure. Full 3-day strength sessions (A, B, C), 90m outdoor ride, and 7.0 mi long run.

Week 2 (Aug 17 – Aug 23): Double-Digit Prep & Fueling Rehearsal

Reasoning: Increase long run to 9.0 miles. 5 mi interval run, 5 mi easy run, 105m long ride. Rehearse gel fueling protocol.

Week 3 (Aug 24 – Aug 30): Peak Volume & Stamina Surge

Reasoning: 10.5 mi long run and 120m peak outdoor ride. 5.5 mi tempo run. Maximum volume week before confidence test.

Week 4 (Aug 31 – Sep 6): 12-Mile Confidence Test & Race Dress Rehearsal

Reasoning: Execute 12.0 mi confidence run on Sat Sep 5. Rehearse exact race-day nutrition (Maurten gels), pacing, and gear.

Week 5 (Sep 7 – Sep 13): Sharpening & Taper Phase 1

Reasoning: Drop volume by 35%. 8.0 mi taper long run, 4.0 mi tempo run. Maintain leg speed while shedding accumulated fatigue.

Week 6 (Sep 14 – Sep 20): Race Week Peak, Carb Load & Orca Half Marathon

Reasoning: Easy 3.0 mi and 2.5 mi runs, 2.0 mi shakeout, carb loading (pasta/rice dinner), 3L hydration, and RACE DAY! Target pace: 10:18/mi!
