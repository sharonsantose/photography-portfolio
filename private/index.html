Training plan

Ambitious workload. This plan assumes consistent running, a comfortable 6-mile base, and no current injury or medical restriction. Protect sleep and omit secondary work when recovery requires it.

Protect the work that matters

  • Default workday: Primary session at 5:45-7:00 AM; easy secondary session at 4:30-6:30 PM.
  • Cut order: Preserve Saturday long run, Wednesday quality, other runs, then Tuesday/Thursday strength.
  • Next preserve the Sunday ride, weekday bikes, swims, Saturday Session C, then row or StairMaster.
  • Never trade sleep for a secondary session, and never move omitted work to another day.
  • In heat, run by effort during cooler hours and use an accessible route with fluids.
  • During race week, taper quality takes priority over the normal hierarchy.

Non-negotiables

  • Stop every set with 2-3 clean repetitions available.
  • Rest 90-120 seconds after Smith and lower-body work; 60-90 seconds after accessories.
  • Do not chase personal records during this block.
  • Race week uses only the short Tuesday and Thursday activations.

Saturday rule

Run first, refuel, rehydrate, and wait at least 4 hours. Skip Session C after a harder-than-planned run, pain, poor refueling, or lingering leg depletion. Never move it to Sunday.

Progression

  • Aug 6-9: 1-2 sets at RPE 5-6.
  • Aug 10-23: Prescribed sets at RPE 6-7.
  • Aug 24-30: Maintain loads; reduce Saturday.
  • Aug 31-Sep 13: Reduced sets, no failure.
  • Race week: Activation sessions only.
