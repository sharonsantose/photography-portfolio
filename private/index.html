🧠 Master Training Philosophy

This 2:15 Half Marathon plan balances aerobic run development with low-impact cardio (swimming and cycling) and structured 3-day strength training. Legs are protected for Saturday long runs, while Sunday swims accelerate metabolic recovery.

1. Saturday Rule
Long runs come first. Strength Session C is completed at least 4 hours post-run only if fully recovered.
2. Sunday Swim Recovery
Sunday easy swim clears metabolic waste and supports PCOS recovery without joint stress.
3. Quality Run Execution
Wednesday tempo/interval runs strictly hit goal pace (10:18/mi) without over-exertion.

Detailed Workout Schedule (Aug 6 – Sep 20, 2026)

💪 3-Day Strength Workout Sessions

Session A · Tuesday (Lower Body Emphasis + Push/Pull)

Primary leg strength session. Complete 2-3 reps in reserve (RPE 6-7/10). Rest 90-120s after lower body.

Session B · Thursday (Moderate Full-Body Work)

Lower leg volume to protect Saturday's long run. Focus on hip thrusts, lunge control, and upper pull.

Session C · Saturday (Upper Body Dominant - Post Long Run)

Run first, refuel, rehydrate, and wait at least 4 hours before lifting. Skip if legs feel depleted.

🛡️ Protect The Work That Matters

Saturday Rule

The long run always comes first. Skip Saturday weights if the run was harder than planned, form deteriorated, pain appeared, refueling is inadequate, or your legs feel depleted 4 hours later.

Fueling & Recovery

Rehearse exact race gels and hydration during long runs. Consume 30-60g carbs/hr for runs >75 min. Post-workout meal should include 20-30g protein.

PCOS & Health Adjustment

If unusual fatigue, cycle changes, or elevated resting heart rate appear together, take an extra recovery day. Pain that alters running form is an immediate stop signal.
